Better Prompts

Turn a rough idea into a useful, constrained business brief.

Try it yourself

Use this to create the starting brief for NearNow.

Act as a startup strategy assistant. Develop a concise concept for a local-product discovery service serving Jersey and Guernsey. The customer problem is uncertainty about which nearby retailers have an item available today. Prioritise click-and-collect or direct retailer contact rather than owning stock. Separate verified facts from assumptions, identify five research questions, list key operational risks, and finish with a 50-word elevator pitch. Do not invent market statistics, retailer partnerships, domains or legal conclusions.
Review point: Check that facts and assumptions are clearly separated before reusing the output.
02

Research & Critique

Test whether the idea is credible, then challenge the assumptions.

Researcher

Opportunity research

Research whether a local-availability catalogue for Jersey and Guernsey is credible. Examine customer need, geography, delivery and collection constraints, likely retailer categories, substitutes, inventory-data challenges and viable revenue models. Prefer official island sources and Microsoft-accessible public sources. Cite every factual claim and place unsupported propositions in a separate assumptions table.

Critical review pass

Act as a sceptical operating partner. Identify the five assumptions most likely to invalidate this model. For each, propose the cheapest test and a measurable pass/fail threshold.
Treat “critical review pass” as a technique, not a guaranteed product or button name.
03

Business Plan

Convert the evidence and brief into reusable business documents.

Word + SharePoint

Build the plan

Create a concise business plan for NearNow CI using the approved idea brief and research. Include: problem, solution, customer, retailer model, operations, data, revenue, marketing, risks, MVP and launch plan. Clearly label assumptions and decisions requiring approval.

Rewrite the MVP for an investor

Rewrite the selected MVP section for a non-technical investor. Keep it under 180 words. Make clear that NearNow CI is initially a discovery and referral service, not a retailer, delivery operator or source of guaranteed real-time stock. Add three measurable pilot outcomes.

Ground an answer in approved knowledge

Using only the NearNow Retailer Knowledge site, explain the onboarding steps, required inventory fields, freshness rules and escalation route. Cite the source file for each answer. If the site does not answer something, say so.

Excel & Analyst

Make fictional inventory data useful and operational.

Excel

Reliable inventory formulas

Gross margin
=([@Price]-[@Cost])
Margin %
=IFERROR(([@Price]-[@Cost])/[@Price],0)
Available
=MAX(0,[@StockQty]-[@ReservedQty])
Freshness
=IF(NOW()-[@LastUpdated]<=1,"Fresh","Check")

Analyse the inventory

Analyse the Inventory table. Identify categories with the strongest combination of availability and gross margin, quantify stale inventory records, find price or stock anomalies, and recommend three operational actions. Explain the calculations and create appropriate charts. Treat all records as fictional demo data.
05

Outlook & Collaboration

Catch up, extract decisions and turn approved actions into work.

Outlook + Teams

Summarise a launch thread and draft a reply

Summarise the decisions, open questions and actions in this thread. Draft a concise reply confirming the pilot will be pickup-first, will use fictional data in the demo, and will not promise real-time availability unless the retailer record is fresh.

Meeting recap

Summarise decisions, unresolved risks and proposed actions. Do not infer attendance or assign ownership unless the transcript explicitly does so.
Invitation data does not prove attendance. Keep ownership tied to what the meeting record explicitly states.
06

Launch Campaign

Turn the plan into an on-brand retailer story.

PowerPoint

Create the retailer deck

Create an eight-slide launch presentation from the approved NearNow idea brief and business plan. Audience: local retailers. Include customer problem, proposition, customer journey, pilot scope, retailer benefits, data requirements, campaign and next steps. Apply the approved organisational Brand Kit, use only approved brand assets and generate speaker notes.

Positioning

“Find it here. Pick it up today.”

Focus on urgent-need shoppers, occasion and gift shoppers, residents supporting local retail, and retailers seeking qualified local demand.

Design the automation

Design an inventory onboarding process for NearNow using Copilot Studio. The retailer uploads a CSV. Validate the schema, detect duplicates and impossible values, prepare approved rows, create an exception report, require an operations reviewer to approve updates, and prepare a draft correction notice for the retailer. Actions must be visible, reversible and reviewed before external communication.

Customer product-finding agent

I need a child's blue raincoat in Jersey today under £45. Find options using only fresh fictional inventory. Show the retailer, area, price, stock-freshness timestamp and source. Do not promise stock, purchase anything, send a message or contact a retailer without asking me first. Prepare a draft reservation request only after I choose an option.
